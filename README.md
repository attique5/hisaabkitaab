# 🏠 Hisaab Kitab

### A Smart & Simple Room, Mess and Expense Management App for Android

Hisaab Kitab is a lightweight, offline Android application designed to help roommates, shared accommodation residents, students, and workers manage their monthly expenses easily and accurately.

Keep track of room rent, daily grocery purchases, mess expenses, utility bills, and individual payments—all in one simple application.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🏠 **Room Management** – Manage roommates and shared rental expenses.
- 🍲 **Mess Management** – Track daily food purchases and monthly mess expenses.
- 👥 **Member Management** – Add, edit, and manage members with joining and leaving dates.
- 💰 **Automatic Calculations** – Calculate individual shares based on participation days.
- ⚡ **Utility Bills** – Split electricity, water, internet, and rent expenses.
- 🧾 **Custom Expenses** – Add additional shared expenses and divide costs between selected members.
- 📊 **Monthly Reports** – View detailed expense summaries and individual balances.
- 💵 **Payment Tracking** – Manage paid amounts, partial payments, and outstanding dues.
- 📲 **WhatsApp Sharing** – Share individual expense statements and monthly grocery lists.
- 📅 **Monthly History** – Access previous months' transactions and reports.
- 🌍 **Multiple Currencies** – Support for OMR, AED, SAR, PKR, INR, USD, and more.
- 💾 **Backup & Restore** – Securely transfer your records to another device.
- 🔒 **Offline Support** – Store and manage your financial records directly on your device.
